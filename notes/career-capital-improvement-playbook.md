## Compound vs abandon

### Double down (compounds)
- Controls judgment under failure
- Data-quality / exception taxonomies
- Finance-task graders with hard limits
- Writing that survives review
- **One** system with users or hard accountability

### Low return (abandon or starve)
- Portfolio polish as main work
- More educational Apple cases as primary proof
- Repo sprawl
- Waitlist without users
- Job-application optimization as the 10-year strategy

### Highest ROE
Getting your gates **broken by reality**, then publishing what you learned.

---

## Signal vs noise (ruthless)

### Signals
- Educational / non-assurance labels
- WP-FIN-001 structure
- Recon before/after + quarantine
- Validate → quarantine → report thesis
- EvalForge as *scoped* experiment
- Shipping public artifacts

### Noise
- Monopoly / “owns the layer” without traction
- Waitlist-as-hero
- Many repos as peacocking
- Trading/quant identity without edge
- Six parallel professional stories
