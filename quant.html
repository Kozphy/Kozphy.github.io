Secondary work / research notes

Questions to ask
before trusting a backtest.

A methodology checklist for reviewing quantitative research. These notes describe review questions, not verified trading performance or commercial deployment.

1. What could the model know?

Check data timestamps, revisions, availability delays, and the boundaries between training and evaluation. Identify leakage before interpreting results.

2. What survives outside the sample?

Review the split method, walk-forward design, overlapping observations, and the number of experiments tried. Inspect unsuccessful runs alongside successful ones.

3. Which costs are included?

State assumptions for spreads, fees, slippage, execution delay, and liquidity. Stress them instead of treating a frictionless backtest as an outcome.

4. What would stop execution?

Define risk limits, approval requirements, monitoring, and a stop procedure. A research result alone does not demonstrate reliable live operation.

Portfolio scope: no returns, client outcomes, sustained operating history, or production-readiness claims are made by this checklist.
