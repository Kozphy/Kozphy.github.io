Questions before
trusting a backtest.

These notes support Risk Advisory and markets-control conversations. They are review questions, not verified trading performance or a commercial track record.

1. What could the model know?

Check timestamps, revisions, availability delays, and the boundary between training and evaluation. Find leakage before interpreting results.

2. What survives outside the sample?

Review split method, walk-forward design, overlapping windows, and how many experiments were tried. Unsuccessful runs matter as much as successful ones.

3. Which costs are included?

State spreads, fees, slippage, delay, and liquidity assumptions. Stress them instead of treating a frictionless backtest as an outcome.

4. What would stop execution?

Define risk limits, approval requirements, monitoring, and a stop procedure. A research result alone does not demonstrate reliable live operation.

Portfolio scope: no returns claim, no client P&L, no production-readiness claim. For engineering detail, review the public FX research mirror separately.
