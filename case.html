How the calculation works

Two bridges, then a scenario.

  1. FCF proxy = operating cash flow − capex.
  2. Net debt = debt − cash and securities.
  3. Project the cash-flow proxy using five explicit annual growth rates.
  4. Discount those cash flows and a Gordon-growth terminal value.
  5. Subtract net debt and divide by the frozen year-end share count.
Model limits matter. CFO − capex is a simplified proxy, not full unlevered FCFF. This is a single-year snapshot with a frozen share count. The scenario tests arithmetic sensitivity; it does not establish an investment conclusion.
