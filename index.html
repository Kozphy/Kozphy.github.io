About

An auditor's instincts, applied to engineering

I come from accounting and compliance-oriented work: tax administration, SAP system support, and regulated workflows where every record has to hold up to review. I now build software with the same instincts: explicit controls, separation of duties, and evidence you can audit.

My public work applies that lens to three areas: keeping CI pipelines and AI coding agents inside bounded, reviewable automation; building financial data and decision systems that fail closed; and quant research infrastructure that rejects leakage and overfitting before capital is at risk.

Technology Risk Lens

01 Fail closed

Automation defaults to reject or escalate. Nothing is approved silently.

02 Evidence over claims

Every run leaves an append-only, replayable audit trail.

03 Separation of duties

The component that evaluates a change is never the one that approves it.

04 Honest scope

Each repository states what is implemented, what is simulated, and what is out of scope.
