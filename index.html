How it works

One workflow design partners buy.

Upload or pipe a run. Deterministic gates fail closed. Exceptions require disposition — not silent drops. Export a workpaper-shaped evidence pack.

1 · Ingest

CSV / ledger extracts, pipeline logs, offline agent answers.

2 · Gate

Rules and graders. Fail closed. No “looks fine” without checks.

3 · Quarantine

Open items with side, amount, disposition — reviewable queue.

4 · Evidence pack

JSON + pack a reviewer can reperform. Timestamped process log.
