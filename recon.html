Interactive demo · under 2 minutes

CSV reconciliation
gate → quarantine → pack.

Load the bundled sample (or upload your own CSVs). Exact match on (value date, amount). Unmatched rows quarantine. Every exception needs a disposition before export.

Label: sample / synthetic data · educational demo · not an assurance service.

Method: exact key match on (value_date, amount_usd); remainder quarantined.

CLI twin: python scripts/recon_validate.py (static metrics only).

What this proves

  • Unmatched keys are listed, not dropped.
  • Export is blocked until each exception has a disposition.
  • Evidence pack includes metrics, exceptions, timestamped log, and SHA-256.

What it does not

  • Not an audit opinion, SOX reliance, or client engagement.
  • Not FX, cut-off, or population/sampling design.
  • Not a claim of production customers or compliance certification.
