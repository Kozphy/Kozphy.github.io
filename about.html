About

Automation without a trail
is untrustable in finance.

Checktrail exists so finance and AI ops teams can fail closed, quarantine exceptions, and hand a reviewer something reperformable — not a screenshot.

What we’re building to sell

Design-partner pilots and, later, team software that produce evidence packs from automation runs.

What we do not sell

Assurance services, audit opinions, full GRC suites, or “AI that replaces judgment.”

Stage

Pre-revenue product surface. Seeking design partners. Public demos are educational / synthetic.
